Siding FAQ

When should damaged siding be replaced instead of repaired?

Replacement may be recommended when damage is widespread, materials are deteriorated or matching repairs would not provide a durable result.

Can siding damage lead to water intrusion?

Yes. Open joints, cracks and missing sections can allow moisture behind the exterior finish and should be evaluated promptly.
