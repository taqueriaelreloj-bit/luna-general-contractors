Therma-Tru

Exterior door systems

An exterior entry consists of the door slab together with a frame, sill, weatherstripping and compatible hardware.

Selection check
Check the rough opening, handing, threshold, exposure, finish and flashing. The complete system matters as much as the visible slab.

Coverage and scope

Therma-Tru offers limited warranty programs by system. Lifetime terminology and enhanced coverage require the specified eligibility and components.

Simpson Strong-Tie

Structural connectors and fasteners

Connectors transfer forces between specified framing members when installed with the correct compatible fasteners.

Selection check
Selection depends on the loads, wood treatment, exposure and connection geometry. Decorative appearance does not establish structural capacity.

Coverage and scope

Product documentation governs the selected connector. A hardware warranty does not replace structural design or cover an unspecified assembly.

Exterior wood and compatible connections

Wood assemblies need a suitable species or treatment, sound supporting members and compatible connectors for the exposure.

Selection check
Review moisture exposure, end grain, ground contact, finish maintenance and fastener corrosion before selecting the lumber.

Coverage and scope

Lumber, finish and hardware can carry different terms. Ask for the actual supplier and component documents instead of assuming lifetime coverage.

Questions for this project

What should be decided before splitting the work into phases?

Agree on safe interim conditions, usable areas, material storage, trade handoffs and how completed work will be protected. Identify any component that must be installed together rather than treating every item as independently schedulable.

What needs to be confirmed before door installation begins?

List the hardware set and preparation responsibility. The written scope should also identify the selected products, work boundaries, required trade coordination and approval steps for changes.

What is included with door installation?
