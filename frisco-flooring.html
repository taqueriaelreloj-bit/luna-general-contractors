Local Project Notes

Transitions Should Be Planned Early in Frisco

Before work begins at a Frisco property, it helps to identify the decisions that affect more than one trade or finish. Floor height can change when old material is removed or a different product is installed. Door clearance, reducers, thresholds, baseboards, quarter round, cabinets, and appliances can all be affected.

Measuring those relationships before installation helps avoid improvised transitions at the end of the job. That planning gives the owner clearer expectations and gives each trade better information before its part of the work begins.
