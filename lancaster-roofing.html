01 / Single-ply membranes

Start with TPO. Compare the alternatives.

These membrane families can look similar from the ground. Their chemistry, seams, accessories and approved assemblies differ.

Illustration · generic material

Thermoplastic polyolefin

TPO

Reference: GAF EverGuard TPO

A thermoplastic sheet membrane with heat-welded seams. The roof assembly may use mechanical attachment, approved adhesives or another specified fastening system. White is one available finish; identify the actual product rather than choosing by color alone.

Compare membrane thickness, attachment, insulation, cover board and perimeter details as one assembly.

For your Lancaster propertyFor TPO replacement, list the temporary materials that must be removed first.

Manufacturer warranty reference15–25 years · listed standard TPO materials

GAF’s published limited material warranty lists standard EverGuard TPO 45 / 60 / 80 mil membranes at 15 / 20 / 25 years, respectively, subject to its terms. Other EverGuard lines have different periods.

Illustration · generic material

Polyvinyl chloride membranes

PVC & PVC KEE

Reference: GAF EverGuard PVC / PVC KEE

PVC is another heat-welded thermoplastic membrane family. KEE-modified products are distinct formulations. Where exhaust, oils or process chemicals may reach the roof, request the manufacturer’s compatibility guidance for the actual exposure.

Name the formulation and accessory system; PVC and TPO are not interchangeable simply because both may look white.

For your Lancaster propertyA PVC alternative should identify permanent drain and penetration details beyond the emergency work.

Manufacturer warranty reference15–25 years · varies by PVC / KEE product

GAF’s limited material warranty lists standard EverGuard PVC 50 mil at 15 years and 60 / 80 mil at 20 years. Listed PVC KEE 50 / 60 / 80 mil periods are 15 / 20 / 25 years. Terms and product identity control.

Synthetic rubber membrane

EPDM

Reference: Carlisle Sure-Seal EPDM

A thermoset rubber membrane used in adhered, mechanically fastened and ballasted assemblies, depending on the selected system. Field seams use specified tapes or adhesives rather than the hot-air welding used for TPO and PVC.

Compare seam accessories, attachment, surfacing and service access together. Confirm the exact membrane and approved assembly.

For your Lancaster propertyFor an EPDM repair, confirm compatibility with the existing surface and any temporary patch material.

Manufacturer warranty reference5–30 years · eligible system programs

Carlisle publishes EPDM system submittal options from 5 through 30 years, with different membrane, attachment and project requirements. A proposed period requires manufacturer eligibility and approval; it is not automatic coverage for every EPDM roof.

02 / The wider material guide

Other commercial roof systems

Open a system to see how it is built and what to compare. Selection depends on the building, its existing condition and the proposed work.

04SBS modified bitumenAsphalt-based sheets+

Reinforced asphalt sheets modified with styrene-butadiene-styrene. Base and cap sheets form a specified assembly, with application methods that vary by product, including approved self-adhered, cold-applied or heat-applied systems.

Compare in the estimate: Identify every ply and its application method. Coordinate the work process with the occupied building below.

Johns Manville SBS systems ↗
05APP modified bitumenAsphalt-based sheets+

Asphalt modified with atactic polypropylene and manufactured as reinforced roofing sheets. Many APP systems use heat application; the approved method depends on the exact sheet and specification.

Compare in the estimate: Compare the complete base-and-cap assembly, substrate and flashing details. Include the required work controls in the proposal.

Johns Manville APP systems ↗
06Built-up roofing (BUR)Multiple-ply asphalt roofing+

A roof membrane built on site from alternating layers of bitumen and reinforcing felts. Surfacing is specified as part of the system; gravel is one possible finish, not the definition of every BUR roof.

Compare in the estimate: Record the number of plies, surfacing, existing layers and removal plan. Review the assembly weight and the condition of the supporting deck.

Johns Manville BUR systems ↗
07Commercial metal roofingPanel systems+

Standing-seam and exposed-fastener panels serve different roof designs. A structural panel system, an architectural panel over a deck and an insulated metal roof panel are distinct assemblies.

Compare in the estimate: Confirm the panel’s slope, support, attachment and thermal-movement requirements. Include curbs, transitions and drainage in the comparison.

MBCI metal roof systems ↗See metal profiles on this page →
08Spray polyurethane foam (SPF)Spray-applied insulation & roofing+

Roofing-grade foam is applied on site and paired with a protective surfacing system. Elastomeric coatings are commonly used to protect the foam from ultraviolet exposure and routine surface wear.

Compare in the estimate: Define substrate preparation, foam and coating specifications, application conditions and future inspection or recoating responsibilities.

SPFA roofing technical resources ↗
09Reinforced liquid-applied membranesPolyurethane, PMMA & related resins+

Compatible resin and reinforcement cure into a continuous waterproofing membrane. Product families include polyurethane and PMMA, with different primers, cure conditions and detailing requirements.

Compare in the estimate: Ask whether the proposal is a complete reinforced waterproofing system, localized flashing or a maintenance coating. Specify the intended use and required build-up.

Sika roofing and waterproofing resins ↗
10Silicone, acrylic & urethane coatingsRestoration of suitable existing roofs+

Coatings may form part of a restoration system over a compatible existing roof. Product chemistry, substrate condition, moisture, adhesion and drainage determine whether the proposal is appropriate.

Compare in the estimate: Identify wet or damaged areas and required repairs before coating. A coating proposal should explain preparation and coverage, not conceal an unsuitable substrate.

GAF commercial roof coatings ↗
11Shingles, tile, slate & other sloped coveringsSteep-slope commercial roofs+

Offices, shops and other commercial buildings can have sloped roofs using asphalt shingles, clay or concrete tile, slate, wood or synthetic coverings where suitable. The building’s use alone does not determine its roof covering.

Compare in the estimate: Select for the measured slope, deck, load and manufacturer instructions. Evaluate transitions to any adjoining low-slope membrane separately.

12Protected membranes, roof gardens & paversSpecialty assemblies above waterproofing+

Vegetation, pavers or protective layers are placed above an underlying waterproofing assembly. These are complete roof configurations; plants, ballast or paving do not replace the waterproofing membrane.

Compare in the estimate: Review structural capacity, drainage, access to the membrane and maintenance. Coordinate any solar equipment with the selected roof system as well.

03 / Beyond the membrane

The roof includes everything beneath and around it.

Four items to keep in the proposal

  1. Existing conditionDeck, roof layers, moisture findings and the basis for repair, removal or recover.
  2. DrainageRoof slope, tapered insulation where specified, drains, scuppers and overflow paths.
  3. Details & accessCurbs, penetrations, edges, walls and protected routes to equipment.
  4. Installation & closeoutApproved assembly, work sequence, product records and manufacturer requirements.

04 / Manufacturer coverage

Ask what the years actually cover.

The GAF periods above refer to limited material coverage for leaks caused by manufacturing defects, subject to exclusions and a prorated remedy. Carlisle’s referenced periods are system-program options. These are different scopes of coverage, not equivalent offers or predictions of roof life.

Request the exact warranty or guarantee form, covered components, required inspections, registration and installer qualifications. Manufacturer coverage and contractor workmanship should be documented separately. References here do not establish Luna’s authorization for a manufacturer program.
