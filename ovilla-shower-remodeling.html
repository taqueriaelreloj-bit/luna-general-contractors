Schluter KERDI

Bonded sheet waterproofing

A sheet membrane can form the waterproof layer beneath a compatible tiled shower assembly.

Selection check
Seams, corners, penetrations, drain connections and the selected mortar must follow the system instructions. Tile and grout alone are not the waterproof layer.

Coverage and scope

KERDI membranes have a 10-year limited warranty; eligible complete-system programs may differ. Confirm all component and installation requirements.

Official reference

Daltile

Ceramic and porcelain tile

Tile options differ in size, finish, water absorption and approved location. A wall-only tile is not automatically suitable for a floor.

Selection check
Review the exact tile’s use rating, wet-area suitability, substrate, grout and movement-joint requirements before selecting the pattern.

Coverage and scope

Daltile’s standard tile warranty is limited; specialty collections and system programs have separate terms. Request the exact product warranty.

Official reference

Simpson Strong-Tie

Structural connectors and fasteners

Connectors transfer forces between specified framing members when installed with the correct compatible fasteners.

Selection check
Selection depends on the loads, wood treatment, exposure and connection geometry. Decorative appearance does not establish structural capacity.

Coverage and scope

Product documentation governs the selected connector. A hardware warranty does not replace structural design or cover an unspecified assembly.

Shower Remodeling FAQ

What is included in a custom shower remodel?

A complete shower remodel may include demolition, framing repairs, plumbing changes, waterproofing, tile installation, niche or bench construction, drain work, fixtures and glass installation.

Can a bathtub be converted into a walk-in shower?

Yes. Many existing tub areas can be converted into walk-in showers after evaluating the drain location, plumbing, wall framing, waterproofing requirements and available dimensions.

How long does a shower remodeling project usually take?

Many custom shower projects take about one to three weeks depending on demolition, repairs, waterproofing, tile complexity, material availability and glass fabrication.
