# Commercial roofing material guide

## Scope and content

Adds `#commercial-roofing` to the 36 existing city roofing pages and `roofing.html`, after the shingle and metal guides. `commercial.html` links directly to the main guide. Existing forms, contact bars and footers remain in place.

The guide covers TPO, PVC/PVC KEE, EPDM, SBS, APP, BUR, commercial metal, SPF, reinforced liquid-applied membranes, restoration coatings, steep-slope coverings and specialty protected/vegetated/paver assemblies. These are broad categories, not a claim that every formulation or historical roof product is interchangeable or offered by Luna.

`content/commercial-roofing-pages.mjs` contains 36 individually authored titles, introductions, planning briefs and 108 distinct membrane-selection notes. Shared material facts are kept consistent in `content/commercial-roofing-systems.mjs`. Manufacturer or industry sources are linked inside the visible guide. Native details disclosures work without JavaScript and preserve all copy in the static HTML.

## Maintenance

After generating the root roofing pages, run:

```sh
node scripts/build-roofing-material-guides.mjs
```

This builds the shingle guide, metal guide, original commercial illustrations and commercial guide in order. To update only commercial copy, use `node scripts/build-commercial-roofing.mjs`. Its input guards require the complete 36-city set, unique editorial fields, 108 unique selection notes and existing image assets before page writes.

## Warranty presentation

The stated GAF periods are limited **material** warranty periods tied to listed products and thicknesses, not NDL system guarantees, expected service lives or Luna workmanship terms. The page explains manufacturing-defect coverage, exclusions and proration. Carlisle periods refer to system programs with separate eligibility and approval requirements; the distinction is visible. No manufacturer certification, stock, blanket weather rating, universal cost saving or program eligibility is claimed.
