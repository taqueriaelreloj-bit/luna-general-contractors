Metal roofing / Arlington, Texas

Make the metal-roof scope clear in the paperwork

When an Arlington project involves insurance documents, distinguish the observed damage from a proposed material upgrade. A metal panel profile, coating and fastening method should be named explicitly so everyone can follow the construction being priced.

Your project, in focus

Keep the existing and proposed materials separate

Record what is on the building today and what the new proposal includes. If metal is an alternative to the original covering, request its cost difference and accessory list in writing. The product choice does not establish insurance coverage.

  1. Record the existing covering and photographed damage.
  2. Identify the panel manufacturer, profile and coating.
  3. Separate upgrade decisions from carrier coverage decisions.

Commercial roofing / Arlington, Texas

Separate commercial storm repairs from system upgrades

An Arlington commercial roof proposal should connect storm observations to specific work quantities. A switch to TPO, PVC or another assembly is a separate material decision that needs its own description and cost.

Project planning

Make the claim scope and construction scope traceable

Keep dated condition photos, the existing roof identification and any proposed upgrade together. Ask which quantities are confirmed and which depend on opening the assembly. Coverage remains a carrier and policyholder decision.

01 / Single-ply membranes

Start with TPO. Compare the alternatives.

These membrane families can look similar from the ground. Their chemistry, seams, accessories and approved assemblies differ.

Illustration · generic material

Thermoplastic polyolefin

TPO

Reference: GAF EverGuard TPO

A thermoplastic sheet membrane with heat-welded seams. The roof assembly may use mechanical attachment, approved adhesives or another specified fastening system. White is one available finish; identify the actual product rather than choosing by color alone.

Compare membrane thickness, attachment, insulation, cover board and perimeter details as one assembly.

For your Arlington propertyAsk which documented areas the TPO replacement price covers and where it ends.

Manufacturer warranty reference15–25 years · listed standard TPO materials

GAF’s published limited material warranty lists standard EverGuard TPO 45 / 60 / 80 mil membranes at 15 / 20 / 25 years, respectively, subject to its terms. Other EverGuard lines have different periods.

Polyvinyl chloride membranes

PVC & PVC KEE

Reference: GAF EverGuard PVC / PVC KEE

PVC is another heat-welded thermoplastic membrane family. KEE-modified products are distinct formulations. Where exhaust, oils or process chemicals may reach the roof, request the manufacturer’s compatibility guidance for the actual exposure.

Name the formulation and accessory system; PVC and TPO are not interchangeable simply because both may look white.

EPDM

03 / Beyond the membrane

The roof includes everything beneath and around it.

Four items to keep in the proposal

  1. Existing conditionDeck, roof layers, moisture findings and the basis for repair, removal or recover.
  2. DrainageRoof slope, tapered insulation where specified, drains, scuppers and overflow paths.
  3. Details & accessCurbs, penetrations, edges, walls and protected routes to equipment.
  4. Installation & closeoutApproved assembly, work sequence, product records and manufacturer requirements.

04 / Manufacturer coverage

Ask what the years actually cover.

The GAF periods above refer to limited material coverage for leaks caused by manufacturing defects, subject to exclusions and a prorated remedy. Carlisle’s referenced periods are system-program options. These are different scopes of coverage, not equivalent offers or predictions of roof life.

Request the exact warranty or guarantee form, covered components, required inspections, registration and installer qualifications. Manufacturer coverage and contractor workmanship should be documented separately. References here do not establish Luna’s authorization for a manufacturer program.

Roofing FAQ

How do I know whether my roof needs repair or replacement?

The decision depends on the roof age, extent of damage, active leaks, shingle condition, decking condition and whether a localized repair can provide a durable result.

Can hail damage be present without an active leak?

Yes. Hail can damage shingles, flashing, vents and soft metals before water enters the building, so an inspection is important after a severe storm.

What is normally included in a full roof replacement?

A typical replacement may include tear-off, underlayment, shingles, starter, ridge caps, flashing, pipe boots, ventilation, cleanup and replacement of damaged decking when approved or required.

Can you work from an insurance roofing estimate?

Yes. We can review the approved construction scope, compare it with visible site conditions and complete the covered roofing repairs or replacement. Coverage decisions remain with the carrier and policyholder.
