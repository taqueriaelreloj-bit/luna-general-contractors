What should a limited repair estimate say about the surrounding finish?

It should identify the retained work, new material, connection detail and finish boundary. A sample helps establish an accepted appearance; an exact match is not assured when the old product or its original condition cannot be reproduced.

What needs to be confirmed before pergolas begins?

List current accessories and future provisions. The written scope should also identify the selected products, work boundaries, required trade coordination and approval steps for changes.

Pergolas FAQ

What is the difference between a pergola and a patio cover?

A pergola typically uses an open rafter design for partial shade, while a patio cover generally has a solid roof for greater weather protection.

Can a pergola be attached to the house?

Yes, depending on the wall, roofline, foundation and structural requirements. Freestanding designs are also available.
