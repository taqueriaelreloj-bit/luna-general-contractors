Frequently Asked Questions

What should Midlothian homeowners check after a hailstorm?

Start with visible roof, gutter, vent, siding and exterior damage, then document the condition with photos before temporary repairs or replacement work begins. We can inspect accessible construction components and prepare a repair scope based on what is found.

Can you coordinate a remodel that uses several trades in Midlothian?

Yes. Projects involving framing, plumbing, electrical, drywall, cabinets, tile, flooring and paint benefit from one coordinated sequence so completed work is not damaged by the next trade.

Do newer Midlothian homes still need detailed pre-remodel inspections?

Yes. Even newer homes can have floor-height changes, framing conflicts, plumbing locations, moisture issues or previous modifications that affect a remodel. Field measurements and existing-condition checks help define the scope before materials are ordered.

Can Luna handle insurance-related build-back work in Midlothian?

Yes. We can document visible construction damage, prepare repair estimates and coordinate approved reconstruction after mitigation or storm damage. Insurance coverage decisions remain with the carrier and policy.
