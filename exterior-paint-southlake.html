Exterior Paint Selection in Southlake, TX

Exterior coatings should match the substrate and withstand North Texas sun, heat, wind and rain.

Exterior coating inspection checklist

  • Siding, stucco, masonry, trim and previously coated surfaces
  • Peeling, chalking, mildew, open joints and failed sealant
  • Rot or movement that paint cannot correct
  • Bare areas and the primer required for each substrate
  • Weather window, masking, landscaping protection and cure time

Identify why the coating failed

Peeling can result from moisture, poor adhesion, incompatible coatings or movement. The estimator should inspect the substrate and nearby joints instead of assuming a new coat will solve the original cause.

Put repairs before finish work

Cleaning and dry time come before scraping, sanding and spot repairs. Failed caulk, loose trim and deteriorated material should be addressed before primer and paint so the proposal distinguishes repair work from coating work.

Match the product to conditions

Product, sheen, application method and number of coats should be written into the scope. Temperature, surface moisture, direct sun and forecast conditions affect scheduling and cure time, so the work plan should follow the selected manufacturer's instructions.
