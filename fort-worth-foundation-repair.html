Foundation Repair FAQ

What are common signs of foundation problems?

Common signs include wall or ceiling cracks, sticking doors, uneven floors, separation around windows and visible exterior brick movement.

Does every crack mean foundation failure?

No. Some cracks are cosmetic or caused by normal movement. A qualified evaluation is needed to determine the cause and appropriate repair.
