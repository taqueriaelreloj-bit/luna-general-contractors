Whole Home Remodeling FAQ

What is included in a whole home remodel?

The scope may include layout changes, kitchens, bathrooms, flooring, drywall, texture, painting, doors, trim, cabinets, lighting, plumbing fixtures and other coordinated improvements selected for the property.

Can a whole home remodel be completed in phases?

Yes. Larger renovations can be divided into logical phases based on budget, material availability, access to the home and the order required for inspections and trade work.

Can homeowners remain in the property during construction?

It depends on the extent of demolition, utility interruptions, dust control and how many rooms are affected. Some phased projects allow partial occupancy, while extensive renovations may require temporary arrangements.

How are multiple trades coordinated during a full renovation?

The project is sequenced so demolition, framing, rough plumbing and electrical, inspections, drywall, cabinets, flooring, painting and finish work occur in the proper order.
