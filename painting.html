Preparation Controls the Finish

Our Painting Quality Process

A professional result depends on the work completed before the finish coat. We build the scope around the existing surfaces instead of treating every room or exterior the same.

  1. Protect & Repair

    Floors, fixtures and adjacent finishes are protected while cracks, holes, loose material and failed caulk are addressed.

  2. Clean & Prepare

    Surfaces are cleaned, sanded or deglossed as needed so primer and finish coatings can bond correctly.

  3. Prime & Paint

    Primer is selected for repairs, stains or difficult substrates before the specified finish coats are applied.

  4. Inspect & Clean

    Coverage, cut lines, touch-ups and the work area are reviewed before protection and remaining materials are removed.
