Bonded sheet waterproofing

A sheet membrane can form the waterproof layer beneath a compatible tiled shower assembly.

Selection check
Seams, corners, penetrations, drain connections and the selected mortar must follow the system instructions. Tile and grout alone are not the waterproof layer.

Coverage and scope

Selection check

Shower Remodeling FAQ

What is included in a custom shower remodel?

A complete shower remodel may include demolition, framing repairs, plumbing changes, waterproofing, tile installation, niche or bench construction, drain work, fixtures and glass installation.

Can a bathtub be converted into a walk-in shower?

Yes. Many existing tub areas can be converted into walk-in showers after evaluating the drain location, plumbing, wall framing, waterproofing requirements and available dimensions.

How long does a shower remodeling project usually take?

Many custom shower projects take about one to three weeks depending on demolition, repairs, waterproofing, tile complexity, material availability and glass fabrication.
