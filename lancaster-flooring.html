Flooring FAQ

Which flooring works well for busy households?

Luxury vinyl plank and quality porcelain tile are popular for active homes because they provide durable surfaces, practical maintenance and a wide range of styles. The best option also depends on moisture exposure, pets, traffic and the condition of the subfloor.

Do floors need leveling before installation?

Some properties require patching, grinding or leveling so the finished floor lies flat and performs correctly. The existing surface and subfloor should be evaluated before installation begins.

Can damaged flooring be repaired without replacing the entire room?

Sometimes. Individual planks, tiles, transitions or small sections may be replaceable when matching material is available and the surrounding floor is still in good condition.

Can you replace flooring after a water-damage loss?

Yes. After the affected area has been stabilized and properly dried, flooring, underlayment, baseboards and related finishes can be replaced as part of the reconstruction scope.

What is included in a complete flooring installation?

A complete scope may include removal, disposal, subfloor preparation, underlayment, flooring installation, transitions, baseboards or shoe molding, caulking and final cleanup, depending on the project.
