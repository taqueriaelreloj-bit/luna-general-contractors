Water extraction, drying and reconstruction by Luna: Our team performs all three stages. We remove standing water, dry the affected areas with equipment selected for the conditions, and complete the agreed reconstruction after source correction and documented drying. Any required specialist assessment or remediation is identified separately in the scope. Equipment selection and drying duration depend on the property.

01

Extract the water

Luna removes standing water from the affected areas. We review access, the reported source and site conditions to establish the extraction scope and any source-control or specialist work required.

02

Dry and check moisture

We perform structural drying with a plan for air movement, dehumidification and moisture checks suited to the materials and conditions. Readings guide the transition to reconstruction.

03

Rebuild the affected areas

After source correction and documented drying, Luna completes the agreed repairs to affected drywall, ceilings, trim, flooring, cabinets and other finishes. Assess countertop and sink connections before cabinet disassembly.

Water Damage Restoration FAQ

Does Luna perform water extraction and drying?

Yes. Luna General Contractors performs water extraction, structural drying and reconstruction. Call to discuss the affected areas, site conditions and current availability; the written scope defines the work and sequence for your property.

What parts of a home can be restored after water damage?

Depending on the approved scope, restoration can include drywall, ceilings, insulation, texture, paint, baseboards, doors, cabinets, engineered wood, laminate, tile and other interior finishes.

Can you coordinate several damaged trades in one project?

Yes. Luna General Contractors can coordinate the reconstruction sequence across drywall, carpentry, painting, flooring, cabinets and related repairs so the affected rooms are rebuilt as one organized project.
