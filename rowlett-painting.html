Which records are useful after the project is complete?

Keep the final scope, approved changes, product labels or model numbers, care information, manufacturer warranty documents and completion photographs. Record the responsible contact for each product and installation issue.

What needs to be confirmed before painting begins?

Approve the repaint boundary and texture sample. The written scope should also identify the selected products, work boundaries, required trade coordination and approval steps for changes.

Painting FAQ

What preparation is included before painting?

Preparation may include protecting floors and furnishings, cleaning surfaces, patching drywall, sanding, scraping loose coatings, caulking gaps and applying primer where needed.

When is primer necessary?

Primer is commonly recommended over new drywall, repairs, stains, bare wood, major color changes and surfaces where adhesion or sealing must be improved.

Do you paint occupied homes and active commercial spaces?

Yes. Work areas, access, ventilation and daily cleanup can be coordinated to reduce disruption while the property remains occupied or in operation.

Can exterior painting include siding, fascia, soffits and trim?

Yes. Exterior scopes can include siding, fascia, soffits, doors, trim and other approved surfaces after repairs and preparation are completed.
