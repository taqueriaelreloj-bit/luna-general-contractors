Roofing FAQ

How do I know whether my roof needs repair or replacement?

The decision depends on the roof age, extent of damage, active leaks, shingle condition, decking condition and whether a localized repair can provide a durable result.

Can hail damage be present without an active leak?

Yes. Hail can damage shingles, flashing, vents and soft metals before water enters the building, so an inspection is important after a severe storm.

What is normally included in a full roof replacement?

A typical replacement may include tear-off, underlayment, shingles, starter, ridge caps, flashing, pipe boots, ventilation, cleanup and replacement of damaged decking when approved or required.

Can you work from an insurance roofing estimate?

Yes. We can review the approved construction scope, compare it with visible site conditions and complete the covered roofing repairs or replacement. Coverage decisions remain with the carrier and policyholder.
