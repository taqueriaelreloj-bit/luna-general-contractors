Kitchen Island Layout Tips in McKinney, TX

A useful kitchen island supports traffic, storage and work zones without crowding appliances or door swings.

What to inspect

Measure clearances around the island with appliance doors and drawers open.

Planning the work

Plan electrical, seating overhang, lighting and any plumbing before cabinets and countertops are ordered.

Avoiding repeat problems

The island size should fit the room rather than forcing an oversized feature into a limited walkway.
