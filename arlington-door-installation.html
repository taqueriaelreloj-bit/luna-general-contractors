Structural connectors and fasteners

Connectors transfer forces between specified framing members when installed with the correct compatible fasteners.

Selection check

How will access and protection work while the property is occupied?

Include a room sequence, daily access route, protection responsibilities and a handoff before each area is returned to use. Confirm these details against the actual scope rather than assuming every phase can happen around normal occupancy.

What needs to be confirmed before door installation begins?

Approve handing and hardware locations in writing. The written scope should also identify the selected products, work boundaries, required trade coordination and approval steps for changes.
