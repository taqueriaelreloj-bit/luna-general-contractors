Water Damage Rebuild Process in Dallas, TX

Reconstruction should begin only after the water source is corrected, affected materials are evaluated and the structure is ready to be closed back up.

Rebuild readiness checklist

  • Water source corrected and affected areas released for reconstruction
  • Damaged materials and repair boundaries documented
  • Framing, insulation and mechanical conditions reviewed
  • Cabinet, drywall, texture, paint and flooring matches confirmed
  • Trade sequence, inspections and final cleaning included in the scope

Confirm the building is ready

Before rebuild, verify that the source is corrected, required removal is complete and framing or cavities are sufficiently dry for reconstruction.

Rebuild in the right order

Framing repairs, insulation, drywall, tape and float, texture, primer, paint, trim, doors, cabinets and flooring must be sequenced to avoid damaging completed work.

Match existing finishes

Texture, paint sheen, trim profiles and flooring transitions should be reviewed carefully because exact matches may depend on age and material availability.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can drywall be installed before everything is dry?

No. Closing damp cavities can trap moisture and create additional problems.

Is mitigation the same as reconstruction?

No. Mitigation focuses on stopping and drying the loss; reconstruction restores removed or damaged building materials.

Protect the cabinet layout during a kitchen loss

Water under a sink or appliance can reach cabinet bases, wall finishes and flooring transitions. Record the existing cabinet dimensions and countertop connections before deciding which components can remain and which require replacement.

Work in an occupied property

If the property remains in use, agree on protected routes, room access, material storage and the point at which each area can be used again. The work plan should identify who moves belongings and how the construction area is separated.

Questions for this project

Does Luna perform water extraction and drying?

Yes. Luna General Contractors performs water extraction, structural drying and reconstruction. Call to discuss the affected areas, site conditions and current availability; the written scope defines the work and sequence for your property.

How will access and protection work while the property is occupied?

Include a room sequence, daily access route, protection responsibilities and a handoff before each area is returned to use. Confirm these details against the actual scope rather than assuming every phase can happen around normal occupancy.

What needs to be confirmed before water damage restoration begins?

Separate cabinet-box damage from door, finish and countertop work. The written scope should also identify the selected products, work boundaries, required trade coordination and approval steps for changes.

Are the listed manufacturers included in every estimate?
