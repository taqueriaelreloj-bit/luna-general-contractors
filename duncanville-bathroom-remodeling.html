Bathroom Remodeling FAQ

How long does a complete bathroom remodel usually take?

The schedule depends on demolition, hidden repairs, plumbing or electrical changes, inspections, waterproofing cure times, tile layout, glass fabrication and material availability. A detailed scope and material plan should be established before construction begins.

Can you convert a bathtub into a walk-in shower?

Yes. A tub-to-shower conversion can include demolition, drain and supply-line adjustments, slope preparation, waterproofing, tile, niches, benches, grab-blocking, fixtures and a framed or frameless glass enclosure.

What waterproofing work is included in a custom tile shower?

The system may include substrate preparation, a shower pan or bonded membrane, sealed corners and penetrations, curb or curbless detailing, flood testing when appropriate, and tile installation over the approved waterproof assembly.

Can the bathroom layout or plumbing locations be changed?

Yes, when site conditions allow. Moving a vanity, toilet, tub or shower may require opening walls or concrete, rerouting supply and drain lines, framing changes, permits and additional repair work.

Do you handle the complete bathroom project or only tile?

Luna General Contractors can coordinate the complete remodel, including demolition, carpentry, plumbing, electrical, waterproofing, tile, glass, vanity installation, countertops, drywall, painting, trim and final finish work.
