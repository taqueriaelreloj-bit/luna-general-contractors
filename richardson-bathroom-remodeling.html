Shower Details That Protect the Remodel in Richardson

For a project in Richardson, the useful starting point is the condition of the actual property and the way the space is used. The shower is one of the most detail-sensitive parts of a bathroom remodel. Drain location, slope, waterproofing, niches, curbs, corners, and glass attachment points all need to work together.

Planning those items before tile installation gives the finish crew better control of cuts and alignment and gives the homeowner a clearer idea of the final layout. The result is a more organized project with fewer assumptions between the owner, contractor, and individual trades.
