Luxury Master Bathroom Remodel in Midlothian

Complete master bathroom transformation featuring a custom walk-in shower, frameless glass, dual vanities, decorative tile, updated lighting and refined trim details.

Call for a Free Estimate

Project Challenge

The existing bathroom had an outdated layout, aging finishes and a shower area that required full demolition before the new design could be built.

Our Solution

Completed demolition, framing and plumbing preparation, then installed a custom tiled shower, bench, niche, frameless glass, new vanities, countertops, lighting, trim and finish work.

Materials and Work Included

  • Large-format porcelain tile
  • Decorative herringbone accent tile
  • Shower pan materials
  • Frameless glass enclosure
  • Custom vanities
  • Stone countertops
  • Plumbing fixtures
  • Lighting fixtures
  • Paint and trim

Project duration: Approximately 4 to 6 weeks

Completed: 2020-08-25
