USG Sheetrock

Gypsum wall and ceiling panels

Gypsum panels provide interior wall and ceiling surfaces. Moisture-resistant and fire-rated products have specific applications.

Selection check
Match thickness, framing, fasteners and assembly requirements. Moisture resistance does not make a board waterproof or suitable for every wet location.

Coverage and scope

Use the selected panel documentation and applicable USG warranty; no universal term applies to every panel or wall assembly.

Questions for this project

How will access and protection work while the property is occupied?

Include a room sequence, daily access route, protection responsibilities and a handoff before each area is returned to use. Confirm these details against the actual scope rather than assuming every phase can happen around normal occupancy.

What needs to be confirmed before commercial construction begins?

Document business access and work-zone boundaries. The written scope should also identify the selected products, work boundaries, required trade coordination and approval steps for changes.

Are the listed manufacturers included in every estimate?

No. These are references for comparing product categories. Availability, suitability, the exact model and the actual supplier must be confirmed for the property. A reference does not imply certification, stock or a brand affiliation.

Project Planning

Keeping Commercial Improvements Practical

A commercial scope may involve correcting damage, updating finishes, adapting a space for a new tenant or improving accessibility. We identify which trades are actually required, what must happen first and which existing materials can remain.

For occupied properties, sequencing can be planned around access and business operations when the project allows it. For vacant spaces, the work can be organized around an efficient construction sequence from demolition through finish work and final cleanup.
