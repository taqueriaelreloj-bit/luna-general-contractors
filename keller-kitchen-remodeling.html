How can two proposals be compared fairly?

Align quantities, preparation, selected product and installation boundaries, then identify exclusions and owner-supplied items. Ask for differences to be explained in writing before deciding which proposal meets the intended result.

Kitchen Remodeling FAQ

What should be planned before a kitchen remodel begins?

The project scope should address layout, cabinets, appliances, electrical, plumbing, countertops, backsplash, flooring, lighting, permits, selections and material lead times before demolition begins.

Can Luna General Contractors coordinate the complete kitchen remodel?

Yes. We can coordinate demolition, framing adjustments, drywall, cabinets, countertops, tile, flooring, painting, finish carpentry and the required plumbing and electrical trades under one organized project.

Can a kitchen remain usable during remodeling?

Limited use may be possible during some phases, but demolition, plumbing, electrical, cabinet and countertop work often require a temporary kitchen arrangement.

Can you install new cabinets without changing the entire floor plan?

Yes. Many projects keep the existing kitchen footprint while replacing cabinets, countertops, backsplash, fixtures, lighting and finishes. Layout changes can also be evaluated when the owner wants an island, pantry or more open design.

Yes. Luna General Contractors serves homes and businesses throughout Keller and nearby communities.
