Concrete Driveways FAQ

What affects the cost of a concrete driveway?

Cost depends on square footage, thickness, reinforcement, demolition, access, grading, drainage, finish and local requirements.

How long before a new driveway can be used?

Curing time varies by conditions and concrete mix. Light foot traffic may be possible sooner, while vehicle use generally requires a longer curing period.
