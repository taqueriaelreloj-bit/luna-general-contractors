Choosing between two complete scopes

Questions for this project

How can two proposals be compared fairly?

Align quantities, preparation, selected product and installation boundaries, then identify exclusions and owner-supplied items. Ask for differences to be explained in writing before deciding which proposal meets the intended result.

What needs to be confirmed before window replacement begins?

State which frame parts will remain. The written scope should also identify the selected products, work boundaries, required trade coordination and approval steps for changes.

Window Replacement FAQ

When should residential windows be replaced?

Replacement may be appropriate when windows leak air or water, have damaged frames, fogged glass, poor operation or no longer meet the homeowner's performance goals.

Can windows be replaced without damaging interior walls?

Many replacements can be completed with limited disturbance, although damaged framing, trim or water intrusion may require additional repairs.
