Siding FAQ

When should damaged siding be replaced instead of repaired?

Replacement may be recommended when damage is widespread, materials are deteriorated, moisture has reached the sheathing or matching repairs would not provide a durable and consistent result.

Can siding damage lead to water intrusion?

Yes. Open joints, cracks, missing panels, failed caulking and damaged trim can allow moisture behind the exterior finish and should be evaluated promptly.

Can you repair soffit, fascia and exterior trim with the siding project?

Yes. Related exterior components can be inspected and included in the same repair scope when they are damaged, deteriorated or contributing to water entry.

Do siding repairs include painting and sealing?

When included in the scope, repaired or replaced areas can be caulked, primed, painted and sealed so the exterior has a finished and weather-resistant result.
