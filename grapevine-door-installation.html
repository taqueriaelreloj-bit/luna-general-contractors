Door Installation FAQ

Can a new door be installed in an existing opening?

Often yes, but the opening must be checked for size, level, rot, framing condition and compatibility with the selected door.

What is included with door installation?

The scope may include removal, fitting, shimming, fastening, hardware, casing, weather sealing, caulking and finish touch-ups.
