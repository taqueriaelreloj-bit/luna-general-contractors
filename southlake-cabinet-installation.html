Questions for this project

Which records are useful after the project is complete?

Keep the final scope, approved changes, product labels or model numbers, care information, manufacturer warranty documents and completion photographs. Record the responsible contact for each product and installation issue.

What needs to be confirmed before cabinet installation begins?

List end panels, trim and toe-kick material. The written scope should also identify the selected products, work boundaries, required trade coordination and approval steps for changes.

What should be completed before cabinet installation?

Measurements, layout, wall condition, plumbing, electrical locations, flooring strategy and appliance dimensions should be confirmed before installation begins.

Can new cabinets be installed in an existing kitchen layout?

Yes. Cabinets can follow the existing layout or be reconfigured when plumbing, electrical, clearances and countertop requirements allow.
