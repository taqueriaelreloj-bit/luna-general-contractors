Roof Ventilation Planning in Plano, TX

Proper roof ventilation can help control attic heat and moisture when intake and exhaust are balanced for the roof design.

What to inspect

Review soffit intake, ridge or roof exhaust, blocked air paths and attic insulation before selecting a correction.

Planning the work

Ventilation should be coordinated with roofing, decking, insulation and bathroom exhaust systems rather than treated as an isolated product.

Avoiding repeat problems

A site inspection is needed because roof shape, attic layout and existing penetrations affect the practical solution.
