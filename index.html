A Clear Construction Process

Large companies often force projects into a standard package. Luna starts with field conditions, the owner's priorities and a written scope that the crews can actually build.

  1. Inspect & Listen

    We review visible conditions, measurements, access, priorities and the way the space needs to function.

  2. Build the Scope

    The estimate identifies the work, major materials, related trades, exclusions and decisions that still need confirmation.

  3. Coordinate the Work

    Crews, materials, protection and trade sequencing are organized around the agreed construction plan.

  4. Final Walkthrough

    We review completed work with the owner, address remaining items and document project completion.

Frequently Asked Questions

Before You Start a Project

Clear expectations make construction easier to compare and manage. These answers explain how Luna approaches work throughout Dallas–Fort Worth.

Request a Free Estimate
What areas does Luna General Contractors serve?

We serve residential and commercial properties throughout Dallas–Fort Worth, including Dallas, Fort Worth, Midlothian, Mansfield, Arlington, Grand Prairie and surrounding DFW communities. See our service areas.

Do you provide written construction estimates?

Yes. We review the property and requested work, then prepare a scope based on visible conditions, measurements, materials, trade coordination and project requirements.

Can Luna coordinate several trades on one project?

Yes. As a general contractor, we can coordinate related work such as framing, roofing, drywall, painting, flooring, carpentry, plumbing and electrical work within one organized construction scope.

Can you help document insurance-related property repairs?

We can document visible damage, measurements and the construction repair scope, prepare estimates and coordinate covered repairs. The insurance carrier and policy determine coverage.

Do you handle residential and commercial construction?

Yes. We work on residential remodeling and repairs as well as commercial improvements, repairs and coordinated build-outs.
