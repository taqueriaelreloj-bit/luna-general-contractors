Questions for this project

How can two proposals be compared fairly?

Align quantities, preparation, selected product and installation boundaries, then identify exclusions and owner-supplied items. Ask for differences to be explained in writing before deciding which proposal meets the intended result.

What needs to be confirmed before backsplash installation begins?

Document the accepted sample and repair limits. The written scope should also identify the selected products, work boundaries, required trade coordination and approval steps for changes.

Are the listed manufacturers included in every estimate?

No. These are references for comparing product categories. Availability, suitability, the exact model and the actual supplier must be confirmed for the property. A reference does not imply certification, stock or a brand affiliation.

Backsplash Installation FAQ

How long does a kitchen backsplash installation usually take?

Many standard backsplash projects can be completed in about two days, although detailed patterns, repairs, specialty materials or extensive cuts may require additional time.

Can backsplash tile be installed around existing cabinets and countertops?

Yes. The layout can be measured and cut around cabinets, countertops, outlets, switches, windows and appliances for a finished installation without replacing the entire kitchen.

What backsplash materials can Luna General Contractors install?

Common options include ceramic and porcelain subway tile, glass mosaic, marble-look tile, hexagon tile, patterned tile and decorative accent pieces.
