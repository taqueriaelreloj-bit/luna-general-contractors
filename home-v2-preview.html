Before You Hire a Contractor

Frequently Asked Questions

What construction services do you provide?

We provide roofing, kitchen and bathroom remodeling, flooring, painting, drywall, siding, carpentry, fencing, water-damage restoration, insurance-claim restoration coordination and commercial construction services.

Do you provide free estimates?

Yes. You can request a free estimate through the website, upload project photos and provide the property address so we can better understand the work.

Can you help with insurance restoration work?

Yes. We can document construction damage, prepare a restoration scope and coordinate the repair and build-back work associated with an insurance claim.

Which areas do you serve?

We serve Dallas, Fort Worth, Midlothian, Mansfield, Arlington, Grand Prairie and many other communities across the Dallas–Fort Worth Metroplex.

Do you handle residential and commercial projects?

Yes. Our work includes residential repairs and remodeling as well as office, retail and light-commercial construction and renovation.
