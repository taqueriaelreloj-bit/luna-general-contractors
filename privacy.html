Privacy Notice

Effective September 3, 2026

Information you submit

When you request an estimate, the form may collect your name, phone number, email address, requested service, project address and any project photos you choose to upload. Required and optional fields are identified in the form.

How the information is used

The information is sent to Luna General Contractors so the team can respond to your request, discuss the property and requested work, schedule an inspection when appropriate and prepare next steps for an estimate.

Website services

The estimate form is processed by Formspree. The website also uses Google Analytics to measure visits and actions such as phone-call and estimate-request clicks. Those providers may process technical information under their own privacy terms.

Your choices

You do not have to upload photos or provide a project address in the first request. You can also contact Luna by phone at (817) 784-5998.

Questions or corrections

To ask about information submitted through the website or request a correction, use the online contact form or call (817) 784-5998.
