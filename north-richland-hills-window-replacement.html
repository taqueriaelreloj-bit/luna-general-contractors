Questions for this project

What should be decided before splitting the work into phases?

Agree on safe interim conditions, usable areas, material storage, trade handoffs and how completed work will be protected. Identify any component that must be installed together rather than treating every item as independently schedulable.

What needs to be confirmed before window replacement begins?

Confirm operating clearances and required opening dimensions. The written scope should also identify the selected products, work boundaries, required trade coordination and approval steps for changes.

Are the listed manufacturers included in every estimate?

Window Replacement FAQ

When should residential windows be replaced?

Replacement may be appropriate when windows leak air or water, have damaged frames, fogged glass, poor operation or no longer meet the homeowner's performance goals.

Can windows be replaced without damaging interior walls?

Many replacements can be completed with limited disturbance, although damaged framing, trim or water intrusion may require additional repairs.
