Drywall Repair After Water Damage in Grand Prairie

Drywall repair after a Grand Prairie water loss should begin only after the source is corrected and cavities are ready for reconstruction.

Drywall repair after a Grand Prairie water loss should begin only after the source is corrected and cavities are ready for reconstruction.

Remove what is necessary

Cut lines should provide access to wet materials and create a practical repair while accounting for insulation, trim, cabinets, and flooring.

Match texture and paint

Texture blending often requires a wider area than the patch, and paint may need to extend to natural breaks.

Check hidden components

Review framing, insulation, wiring, plumbing, and remaining signs of moisture before closing the wall.

The actual scope depends on the property and requested materials. Luna General Contractors can review visible conditions and prepare a project estimate.
