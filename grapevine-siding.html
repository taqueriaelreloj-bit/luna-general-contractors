What happens if removal reveals additional work?

Record the finding and its effect on the proposed construction. Obtain written approval for the changed scope, cost and schedule before proceeding with work beyond the agreement, apart from any separately authorized protection.

What needs to be confirmed before siding and exterior cladding begins?

Keep the exact product and care instructions in the closeout file. The written scope should also identify the selected products, work boundaries, required trade coordination and approval steps for changes.
