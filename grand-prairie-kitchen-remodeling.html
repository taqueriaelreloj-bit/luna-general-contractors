Kitchen Remodeling FAQ

What should be planned before a kitchen remodel begins?

The scope should address layout, cabinets, appliances, electrical, plumbing, countertops, flooring, lighting, permits and material lead times.

Can a kitchen remain usable during remodeling?

Limited use may be possible during some phases, but demolition, plumbing and countertop work often require a temporary kitchen arrangement.
